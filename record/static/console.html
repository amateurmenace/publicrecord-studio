Municipality

The night, as it stands

Four things happen at night, each on the one before: the poll files and approves, the pipeline ingests, the edition is pressed and carried, the embed job catches meaning-search up. This screen reads the platform directly — what ran, whether it finished, and its own sentences.

What the record is allowed to swallow

Default-deny. A video enters the queue only if a rule names the public body it belongs to. Nothing here spends anything until you press Poll now — preview is a pure function over the feed and writes nothing.

Submissions

Nothing ingests on the strength of a stranger's POST. Approving marks the row for the pipeline job; it does not transcribe a meeting while you wait.

Meetings on the record

Every meeting the pipeline has touched, newest day first — live, parked without words, or mid-flight — with how much of it meaning-search can see. A live meeting links to its own page in the pressed edition.

Curation

The desk's verbs, over HTTP, with a name against every one. Each runs in a transaction and writes an audit row.

Verbs that do not belong to one issue

Mint and rebuild both act on the town selected above. Rebuild re-derives links from segments; it keeps minted, steward-touched and followed issues.

The audit log

The record remembers its own edits — a name and a time against each one. A standing rule signs as rule:<the source>; a job run from this desk signs with the steward who pressed the button.

What a job said

Pick an execution on the Tonight screen, or name one here, to read the last lines it printed.

Spend

The AI-audit ledger, ported up from the desk: every token attributed, visible here rather than in a bill nobody reads. The server's spend is the project's — a reader's identity is never part of the price.

What the night runs on

The switches a steward flips live on the Intake screen, per source. Everything here is the service's own configuration — read from the running service, set where the note beside it says — so the screen never disagrees with the thing that runs at three in the morning.
